Hamrahe is a professional networking platform for individuals, companies, startups, teams, recruiters, employees, job seekers, and business partners.
It is in the same broad category as LinkedIn, but it must not copy LinkedIn.
The Company Profile must be more intelligent, more trustworthy, more localized for the Iranian market, and more deeply integrated with jobs, learning, assessments, events, people, content, and trust.
