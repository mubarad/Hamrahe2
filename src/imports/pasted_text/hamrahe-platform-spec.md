Design a modern, scalable, bilingual (Persian/English) professional social networking platform called "Hamrahe" (temporary name).

The product is a mobile-first responsive web app (convertible to mobile app), inspired by LinkedIn but redesigned with a cleaner, more engaging, social-first UX and optimized for the Iranian market.

---

## Core Concept

Hamrahe is a professional social network focused on:

- Social interaction
- Job discovery and hiring (primary revenue driver)
- Professional identity and trust
- Progressive profile completion

Learning will be introduced in future phases and should NOT be implemented in this version (only a placeholder).

---

## Target Users (MVP)

- Job seekers
- Mid to senior professionals
- Companies and HR teams

---

## Design Style

- Inspired by LinkedIn, but more modern, lighter, and more social
- Minimal, structured, high readability
- Avoid overly corporate stiffness
- Soft shadows, rounded corners, clear spacing
- Smooth UX similar to modern SaaS products

---

## Theme

- Light Mode + Dark Mode
- Suggested palette:
  - Primary: modern deep blue (LinkedIn-inspired, refined)
  - Neutral gray system
  - Accent: cyan / electric blue

- Accessible contrast in both modes

---

## Language

- Fully bilingual:
  - Persian (RTL)
  - English (LTR)

- Proper RTL/LTR switching
- Typography must support both cleanly
